# Resume Tailoring Instructions

These instructions are applied during every `/forge-tailor` run. Use them to
correct, constrain, or clarify how bullets and fields may be rephrased —
especially to prevent fabricated or exaggerated claims.

---

## Hard constraints (never violate)

- Do NOT invent metrics, percentages, dollar amounts, or timelines that are not
  already present in the base resume. If a bullet says "$XXXM", leave the
  placeholder or omit the figure — do not substitute a specific number.
- Do NOT add technologies, frameworks, or tools to a bullet unless they already
  appear in that bullet or in the skills list.
- Do NOT change job titles, company names, employment dates, or degree
  information.
- Do NOT merge or split work history entries.

## Phrasing corrections

- Prefer active, first-person-implicit verbs (Led, Built, Designed) over passive
  constructions (Was responsible for, Helped with).
- Do not add superlatives ("world-class", "cutting-edge", "revolutionary") that
  aren't in the source.
